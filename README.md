Coffee Shop Sales Analysis
Project Overview

The main objective of this project is to analyze retail sales data to gain actionable insights that can enhance the performance of a coffee shop. By exploring sales trends, customer preferences, and product performance, this project aims to provide data-driven recommendations to improve business operations, increase revenue, and optimize customer experience.

Objectives

Analyze historical sales data to identify patterns and trends.

Determine the best-selling products and peak sales periods.

Understand customer preferences and behavior.

Identify opportunities to increase revenue and improve operational efficiency.

Provide actionable insights for marketing, inventory, and pricing strategies.

Dataset

The dataset used in this project includes:

Transaction details (date, time, product sold, quantity, price)

Sales amounts and revenue per transaction

Customer demographics (if available)

Product categories

Note: Ensure that the dataset is cleaned and preprocessed before analysis to handle missing values, duplicates, and inconsistencies.

Key Features

Sales Trend Analysis: Examine daily, weekly, and monthly sales patterns.

Product Performance: Identify top-selling and underperforming items.

Customer Insights: Analyze purchase behavior and preferences.

Revenue Forecasting: Predict future sales to aid in planning and inventory management.

Visualizations: Charts and graphs to simplify insights and trends.

Tools & Technologies
Python (Pandas, NumPy, Matplotlib, Seaborn)
Jupyter Notebook
Excel / CSV for dataset handling

Conclusion

This project provides a comprehensive analysis of the coffee shop’s sales data, offering actionable insights to improve sales, optimize inventory, and enhance customer satisfaction. The findings can help management make informed business decisions to drive growth and profitability.
